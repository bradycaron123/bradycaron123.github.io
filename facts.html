Here are some fun facts about the city of Los Angeles

  • The city’s original name was El Pueblo de Nuestra Señora la Reina de los Ángeles.
  • LA is home to Hollywood and the iconic Hollywood Sign. The sign originally read “Hollywoodland” when it was built in 1923 as a real estate advertisement.
  • Before becoming part of the U.S. in 1848, Los Angeles was part of Mexico for 27 years.
  • The Hollywood Walk of Fame features over 2,700 stars embedded in the sidewalks.
  • The iconic palm trees are not native to Los Angeles and were imported, primarily for landscaping in the early 20th century.
  • The Santa Monica Pier is home to the world's only solar-powered Ferris wheel.
